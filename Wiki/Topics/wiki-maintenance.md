# Wiki maintenance

## Overview

Maintaining an LLM Wiki includes rebuilding the catalog and indexes, validating compiled-note metadata and source links, checking whether raw sources are covered, and auditing for accidental public exposure of secrets or private machine state.

## Routine checks

The setup guide defines deterministic commands for health checks, building indexes, linting notes, scanning sources, checking source coverage, searching the catalog, and appending a log entry.
